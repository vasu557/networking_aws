how switch works : 
red - icmp (second - unicast) - ping (internet control messaging protocol)
blue - arp (first - broadcast) -  (address resolution protocol)
resolution - mapping mac address to ip address
       - arp packet doesn't have any information.
it broadcast to every one and bring back the mac address of destination device and gives to switch. 
      - and then switch send the data to destination computer by icmp packet.

=>total ports in switches - 24
out of 24 - 2 are gB ports
  ports or interfaces both are same

switches are two types :
 unmanaged switch - no configuration -  we can't change the switch settings
  in real time these unmanaged switches are not used because to work with switches it must be configured(managed).
managed switches - customized switches
                                 we can able to configure these managed switches.
                                 - these managed switches are works in real time
